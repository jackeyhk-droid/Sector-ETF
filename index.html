兩層分析框架Two-Tier Framework

關鍵洞察：並非所有「板塊 ETF」對標普500的關係都相同。精選板塊是指數的「切片」；主題型 ETF 則是跨越指數邊界的「籃子」。Key insight: not all "sector ETFs" relate to the S&P 500 the same way. Select Sectors slice the index; thematic ETFs are baskets that cross its boundary.

主動份額（Active Share）的真相：即使名稱重疊，權重結構完全不同。NVDA 佔標普500約 7.5%，但佔 SMH 達 15.1%、SOXX 7.0%。以 Active Share = ½Σ|w_ETF − w_基準| = 1 − Σmin 計算，五檔落在 45–98%（QQQ 45.2%、SMH 83.0%、SOXX 85.3%、IGV 91.6%、ITA 98.0%）——並非 100%：因為 NVDA 等大型股在 ETF 與指數中同時佔高權重，重疊部分相互抵銷而壓低主動份額。QQQ 最低（95% 權重在指數內，但對 NVDA 等大型股大幅超配，偏離仍達 45%）、ITA 最高。重疊看的是「持有什麼」，主動份額看的是「偏離多少」。The truth about active share: even where names overlap, the weight structure differs entirely. NVDA is ~7.5% of the S&P 500 but 15.1% of SMH and 7.0% of SOXX. Using Active Share = ½Σ|w_ETF − w_bench| = 1 − Σmin, the five funds span 45–98% (QQQ 45.2%, SMH 83.0%, SOXX 85.3%, IGV 91.6%, ITA 98.0%) — not 100%: mega-caps like NVDA carry high weight in both the ETF and the index, so the overlapping part nets out and pulls active share down. QQQ is lowest — 95% of its weight is in the index, yet it deviates 45% through heavy mega-cap overweighting; ITA highest. Overlap is what you hold; active share is how much you deviate.

方法論與資料來源Methodology & Sources

// DEFINITIONS

  • % 標普500：該 GICS 板塊佔標普500的市值權重（精選板塊指數口徑）。% of S&P 500 — each GICS sector's cap-weight within the index (Select Sector index basis).
  • 權重重疊：ETF 中屬於標普500成分股的權重百分比（最具經濟意義的口徑）。Weight overlap — the share of an ETF's weight held in S&P 500 constituents (the economically meaningful measure).
  • 成分重疊：ETF 持股中屬於標普500成分股的「家數」。Count overlap — the number of an ETF's holdings that are S&P 500 constituents.
  • 主動份額（Active Share）：相對基準的權重偏離，= ½Σ|w_ETF − w_基準| = 1 − Σmin(w_ETF, w_基準)。本表五檔為 45–98%（非 100%，大型股重疊抵銷；QQQ 最低、ITA 最高）。Active Share — weight deviation from the benchmark, = ½Σ|w_ETF − w_bench| = 1 − Σmin. The five funds here compute to 45–98% (not 100%, as mega-cap overlap nets out; QQQ lowest, ITA highest).
  • 追蹤誤差（Tracking Error / 主動風險）：ETF 相對基準報酬的年化標準差，TE = √(σ_E² + σ_B² − 2ρσ_Eσ_B)。數值越高，偏離標普500的波動代價越大。Tracking Error (active risk) — annualised std-dev of ETF-minus-benchmark return, TE = √(σ_E² + σ_B² − 2ρσ_Eσ_B). Higher = greater volatility cost of deviating from the S&P 500.
  • 集中度：有效持股數 EffN = 1/Σwᵢ²（HHI 倒數）與前五大權重。EffN 越低＝越集中（如 ITA EffN≈10、前五大 53.5%）。Concentration — effective holdings EffN = 1/Σwᵢ² (inverse HHI) and top-5 weight. Lower EffN = more concentrated (e.g., ITA EffN≈10, top-5 53.5%).

// CAVEATS

  • 揭露持股口徑：SMH 為完整25檔、QQQ 為完整 101 檔（那斯達克100）；SOXX／ITA／IGV 取前25大持股（分別涵蓋 96.2% / 95.6% / 85.3% 權重）。權重重疊穩健；「指數外」家數含未揭露尾端（多為小型股，多數在指數外）。各 ETF 總持股數：SMH 25、SOXX 34、ITA 48、IGV 116、QQQ 101。主動份額／追蹤誤差亦以此基礎估算。Disclosed-holdings basis: SMH is the full 25 and QQQ the full 101 (Nasdaq-100); SOXX / ITA / IGV use the top-25 (covering 96.2% / 95.6% / 85.3% of weight). Weight overlap is robust; the "outside" count omits the undisclosed tail (mostly sub-scale, largely outside the index). Total holdings: SMH 25, SOXX 34, ITA 48, IGV 116, QQQ 101. Active share and tracking error are estimated on the same basis.
  • 外國 ADR 不屬標普500：台積電（TSM）、ASML、Arm、意法（STM）、ASE（ASX）因註冊地非美國而被排除。Foreign ADRs are not in the S&P 500: TSMC (TSM), ASML, Arm, STMicro (STM) and ASE (ASX) are excluded by domicile.
  • MRVL（Marvell）將於 2026-06-22 納入標普500（季度再平衡），截至本報告日仍未納入，故計為「指數外」。Marvell (MRVL) joins the S&P 500 on 2026-06-22 (quarterly rebalance); as of this report it is not yet a member and is counted as outside.
  • 單一時點：重疊與主動份額為當前斷面（point-in-time）。歷史滾動重疊（如近 3 年趨勢）需逐期持股時間序列，現有資料源未涵蓋，列為後續擴充。Point-in-time: overlap and active share are a current snapshot. A historical rolling overlap (e.g., a 3-year trend) needs a period-by-period holdings time series not available from current sources — flagged as a future enhancement.

// PORTFOLIO LENS

  • 持有 NVDA / MU / AMD 者，已透過 VOO 取得其指數權重；SMH／SOXX 會大幅放大同一曝險（NVDA 在 SMH 達 15.1%）。If you hold NVDA / MU / AMD, you already own their index weight via VOO; SMH / SOXX greatly amplify the same exposure (NVDA is 15.1% of SMH).
  • SMH 與 SOXX 彼此重疊度極高——兩者並持的分散效益有限，差異主要在權重與外國股比例。SMH and SOXX overlap heavily with each other — holding both adds little diversification; the difference is mainly weighting and foreign-stock share.
  • ITA 的「指數外」權重最高（含 RKLB、KTOS、AVAV、HEICO、Curtiss-Wright）——是取得標普500之外國防曝險的工具。ITA has the largest out-of-index weight (Rocket Lab, Kratos, AeroVironment, HEICO, Curtiss-Wright) — a tool for defense exposure beyond the S&P 500.
