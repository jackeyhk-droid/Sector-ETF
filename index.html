板塊與主題型 ETF 對標普500 的重疊分析How much of each sector / thematic ETF duplicates the S&P 500?

以 VOO（Vanguard S&P 500）為基準。Tier 1 為 11 檔 SPDR 精選板塊 ETF——本質上是把標普500按 GICS 切片，故重疊≈100%，分析重點在於「各板塊佔標普500的比重」。Tier 2 為 5 檔（主題型 SMH／SOXX／IGV／ITA + 廣基型 QQQ／那斯達克100），會延伸到指數外，因此進行真正的重疊分析（成分數、權重重疊、指數外名單）。Base = VOO (Vanguard S&P 500). Tier 1 = the 11 SPDR Select Sector funds — the S&P 500 partitioned by GICS, so overlap is ≈100% by construction and the metric becomes each sector's share of the index. Tier 2 = five ETFs — four thematic (SMH / SOXX / IGV / ITA) plus the broad-index QQQ (Nasdaq-100) — that reach outside the index, where genuine overlap analysis applies (count, weight overlap, names outside).

兩層分析框架Two-Tier Framework

關鍵洞察：並非所有「板塊 ETF」對標普500的關係都相同。精選板塊是指數的「切片」；主題型 ETF 則是跨越指數邊界的「籃子」。Key insight: not all "sector ETFs" relate to the S&P 500 the same way. Select Sectors slice the index; thematic ETFs are baskets that cross its boundary.

Track industry-theme indices; they include foreign ADRs (TSMC, ASML, Arm) and sub-scale names not yet in the S&P 500 — so genuine out-of-index exposure exists. QQQ (Nasdaq-100) is added as a broad-index reference — ~91% of weight is in-index, yet active share is just 46% (the most S&P-like).

// DEFINITIONS

  • % 標普500：該 GICS 板塊佔標普500的市值權重（精選板塊指數口徑）。% of S&P 500 — each GICS sector's cap-weight within the index (Select Sector index basis).
  • 權重重疊：ETF 中屬於標普500成分股的權重百分比（最具經濟意義的口徑）。Weight overlap — the share of an ETF's weight held in S&P 500 constituents (the economically meaningful measure).
  • 成分重疊：ETF 持股中屬於標普500成分股的「家數」。Count overlap — the number of an ETF's holdings that are S&P 500 constituents.
  • 主動份額（Active Share）：相對基準的權重偏離，= ½Σ|w_ETF − w_基準| = 1 − Σmin(w_ETF, w_基準)。本表五檔為 （非 100%，大型股重疊抵銷； 最低、 最高）。Active Share — weight deviation from the benchmark, = ½Σ|w_ETF − w_bench| = 1 − Σmin. The five funds here compute to (not 100%, as mega-cap overlap nets out; lowest, highest).
  • 追蹤誤差（Tracking Error / 主動風險）：ETF 相對基準報酬的年化標準差，TE = √(σ_E² + σ_B² − 2ρσ_Eσ_B)。數值越高，偏離標普500的波動代價越大。Tracking Error (active risk) — annualised std-dev of ETF-minus-benchmark return, TE = √(σ_E² + σ_B² − 2ρσ_Eσ_B). Higher = greater volatility cost of deviating from the S&P 500.
  • 集中度：有效持股數 EffN = 1/Σwᵢ²（HHI 倒數）與前五大權重。EffN 越低＝越集中（如 、前五大 ）。Concentration — effective holdings EffN = 1/Σwᵢ² (inverse HHI) and top-5 weight. Lower EffN = more concentrated (e.g., , top-5 ).

  • 外國 ADR 不屬標普500：台積電（TSM）、ASML、Arm、意法（STM）、ASE（ASX）因註冊地非美國而被排除。Foreign ADRs are not in the S&P 500: TSMC (TSM), ASML, Arm, STMicro (STM) and ASE (ASX) are excluded by domicile.
  • 指數變動追蹤：自上次更新（06-05）起，標普500共發生 4 次已生效變動（MRVL 06-22 季度再平衡；HONA／CAG 06-30；FERG／EA 08-05；RDDT／AVB 08-18）與 1 次待生效再平衡（09-21：BE、P、ILMN 納入；TAP、TTD、BLDR 剔除），完整紀錄見 §02 變動表；已生效者均已反映於即時成分數據。SPCX（SpaceX）於 06-12 上市後快速納入那斯達克100（4.67%），但尚未符合標普500的獲利／季化要求，故對 QQQ 計為「指數外」。Index-change tracking: since the last refresh (06-05) the S&P 500 has had four effective changes (MRVL 06-22 quarterly rebalance; HONA / CAG 06-30; FERG / EA 08-05; RDDT / AVB 08-18) and one pending rebalance (09-21: BE, P, ILMN in; TAP, TTD, BLDR out) — full log in §02; effective changes are already in the live constituent data. SPCX (SpaceX) fast-entered the Nasdaq-100 after its 06-12 IPO (4.67%) but does not yet meet the S&P 500's profitability / seasoning rules, so it counts as outside the index for QQQ.
  • 單一時點：重疊與主動份額為當前斷面（point-in-time）。歷史滾動重疊（如近 3 年趨勢）需逐期持股時間序列，現有資料源未涵蓋，列為後續擴充。Point-in-time: overlap and active share are a current snapshot. A historical rolling overlap (e.g., a 3-year trend) needs a period-by-period holdings time series not available from current sources — flagged as a future enhancement.

// PORTFOLIO LENS

  • 持有 NVDA / MU / AMD 者，已透過 VOO 取得其指數權重；SMH／SOXX 會大幅放大同一曝險（NVDA 在 SMH 達 ）。If you hold NVDA / MU / AMD, you already own their index weight via VOO; SMH / SOXX greatly amplify the same exposure (NVDA is of SMH).
  • SMH 與 SOXX 彼此重疊度極高——兩者並持的分散效益有限，差異主要在權重與外國股比例。SMH and SOXX overlap heavily with each other — holding both adds little diversification; the difference is mainly weighting and foreign-stock share.
  • ITA 的「指數外」權重最高（含 RKLB、KTOS、AVAV、HEICO、Curtiss-Wright）——是取得標普500之外國防曝險的工具。ITA has the largest out-of-index weight (Rocket Lab, Kratos, AeroVironment, HEICO, Curtiss-Wright) — a tool for defense exposure beyond the S&P 500.
